Who We Serve

Dignified support for diverse paths to stability

We house and support individuals at every stage of rebuilding — referred directly or through a trusted partner.

Reentry

Individuals returning from incarceration seeking a structured environment to rebuild their lives and achieve long-term independence.

Veterans

Those who served our country and deserve stable, dignified housing as they transition back to civilian life.

Adults with Disabilities

Individuals receiving SSI, SSDI, or private disability benefits who need an accessible, supportive environment.

Elderly Adults

Seniors seeking a safe, furnished, community-centered home with access to care and daily living assistance.

Hospital Referrals

Patients transitioning from medical or behavioral health facilities who need stable housing for continued recovery.

Case Manager Referrals

We partner with social workers, parole officers, and VA coordinators for seamless, trusted client placements.

Referral Process

Simple. Fast. Supportive.

We make it easy for case managers, parole officers, VA coordinators, and hospital discharge planners to refer clients.

  1. Contact Us

    Call or text +1 (225) 505-3100, email info@purposepathhousing.com, or fill out our Client Screening Form below.

  2. Submit Referral Info

    Provide basic client details, income documentation, and any special needs or supervision requirements.

  3. Application Review

    We review promptly and follow up within 1–2 business days with a decision and clear next steps.

  4. Move-In Coordination

    Once approved, we coordinate move-in logistics to ensure a smooth, dignified transition for your client.
