Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Purpose Path Housing LLC ("Purpose Path Housing," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. This policy applies to purposepathhousing.com and does not extend to third-party sites we may link to.

Information We Collect

When you use our Client Screening Form or contact us by phone or email, we may collect information you choose to provide, such as:

  • Your name and, if applicable, your organization
  • Contact information, including email address (and phone number, if you contact us directly by phone or text)
  • The type of referral you are submitting (self-referral, case manager, parole or probation officer, VA coordinator, hospital discharge planner, or other)
  • Any additional notes you choose to include about the referral

We do not use cookies or analytics tools to track visitors beyond what is required for the site to function, unless a specific tool is disclosed elsewhere on this page.

How We Use Information

We use the information submitted through this website solely to:

  • Respond to referral inquiries and follow up on submitted applications
  • Coordinate move-in logistics for approved residents
  • Communicate with case managers, referral partners, and prospective residents about housing availability

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

How We Share Information

We may share the information you provide with staff and partners directly involved in reviewing and processing your referral, such as case management or home care partners named elsewhere on this site, only as needed to evaluate or support a housing placement.

Data Retention

We retain referral and contact information for as long as reasonably necessary to process referrals, maintain resident records, and comply with applicable legal or recordkeeping obligations.

Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we have on file for you, to request a correction, or to request deletion where applicable, using the contact details below.
